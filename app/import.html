Import your reading history

Upload a Goodreads library export (or a Fable library exported via a third-party tool into the same format — Fable itself doesn't offer an export yet) and we'll match it against our catalog.

Goodreads: on a computer, go to My Books → Tools → Import and export → Export Library, then upload the CSV it downloads.

This only imports YOUR OWN ratings into YOUR OWN account here — nothing is shared with anyone else.

Matching your books against the catalog — this can also take up to a minute if the import service was idle. Hang tight…
